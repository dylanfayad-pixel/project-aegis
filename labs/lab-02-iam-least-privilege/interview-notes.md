# Interview Notes

## What is IAM?

AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls who can access AWS resources and what actions they are allowed to perform.

---

## What is Least Privilege?

Grant identities only the permissions required to perform their job.

---

## Why use Groups?

Groups simplify permission management by assigning policies to multiple users.

---

## Why create a test user?

To verify permissions behave as expected without affecting administrator accounts.

---

## What is a customer-managed policy?

A policy created and maintained by an organization rather than AWS.
